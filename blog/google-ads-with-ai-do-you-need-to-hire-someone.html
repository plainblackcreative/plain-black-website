Can AI run your Google Ads, or do you need to hire someone?

4 min read By PlainBlack 15 October 2026
Back to Blog

Short answer: Google already runs a lot of your account with AI. What it can't do is know what a good job is worth to you, or notice when it's being fed the wrong numbers. That's the part you either learn or hire.

The short version

AI does the bidding and the wording. You own the goal and the numbers.

Google's Smart Bidding sets bids, and campaign types like Performance Max and AI Max for Search write headlines and choose which searches to show for. That's built in, and you can switch it on without anyone's help.

It only works as well as what you give it: conversion tracking that counts real enquiries, landing pages that are accurate, and a budget and goal that make sense for your business. Checking those is the job, whoever does it.

See the five questions

What Google's AI already does

  • Bidding. Smart Bidding uses Google AI to bid for conversions or conversion value in each auction. Target CPA, target ROAS, maximise conversions and maximise conversion value are all Smart Bidding.
  • Writing ads. In Performance Max, with text customisation on, Google writes headlines and descriptions from your landing page, your domain and your current ads. AI Max for Search does the same for Search campaigns.
  • Choosing searches and pages. AI Max can match your ads to searches beyond your keywords, and send people to a different page on your site than the one you picked. Performance Max spreads one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps.
  • Changing your account. If automatically applied recommendations are on, Google applies its own suggestions on a regular schedule. You can review the queue or switch it off.

So "can you do Google Ads with AI?" is mostly answered already. If you take the defaults, you're using it.

What it can't do for you

Google's own help pages hand a few jobs back to you.

  • Tell it what counts. Smart Bidding needs conversion tracking switched on, and Google says some strategies work best with a minimum amount of conversion history. If a "conversion" is a page view or a tap that isn't a real enquiry, the AI chases the wrong thing very efficiently.
  • Keep the pages right. When Google picks the landing page for you, it says you're still responsible for reviewing that page for accuracy. If an ad lands someone on an old price, that's yours to fix.
  • Set the limits. You choose the budget, the locations, the brands you don't want to appear beside and the pages it may never use. In AI Max you can turn off the ad writing and the page picking separately.
  • Judge the result. Google says advertisers using AI Max typically see 14% more conversions or conversion value at a similar cost, from its own 2025 data for non-retail advertisers. That's Google's average, not a forecast for your business.

Do you need to hire someone? Five questions

If you can answer yes to most of these, you can probably run it yourself. If you can't, that's the work worth paying for, whether it's us or someone else.

  • Do you know roughly what an enquiry is worth to you, and how many turn into jobs?
  • Is your conversion tracking counting real calls, forms and bookings, and nothing else?
  • Do the pages your ads land on say what the ad says, with current prices and services?
  • Do you look at the searches your ads actually showed for, and exclude the ones you don't want?
  • Do you check what automatically applied recommendations changed, or have you switched them off?

Using ChatGPT or Claude to write ad copy is fine for a first draft. It can't see your account's searches or results unless you paste them in, so on its own it's guessing at what works.

How we handle it

For clients whose ads we run, the Google Ads mod on their PlainBlack dashboard shows what they spent, the calls and enquiries it brought and what we're changing next, with figures updated every day and a short report each month. The owner can check the numbers without logging in to Google Ads.

We don't promise a cost per lead or a number of jobs. Nobody can say that honestly before looking at your tracking, your pages and your market.
